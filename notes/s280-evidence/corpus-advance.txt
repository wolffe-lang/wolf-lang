Why
g1's environment exported WOLF_STD=/lane/wolf-std/std for the whole
gauntlet. conform-run takes its std root from --std-root, then from
WOLF_STD (effective_std_root, crates/wolf_driver/src/main.rs:567;
conform_run, :4186). The corpus step passes --std-root only for a
fixture with an adjacent std/ directory (corpus::fixture_std_root,
xtask/src/corpus.rs:443, used at xtask/src/main.rs:584). Every other
fixture inherits the environment, so the global export became its std
root. method_home_no_std.lu states its own premise: "`any` ... comes
from `std.list`, and no std root is configured for this directory:
E0301". With a root configured, `xs.any` resolves, and the file lowers
through WIR. strings.lu also reaches wir when the root is configured.

The lane did not cause it. Neither file differs between the base
5f1f1c94 and 9973f2b3 (`git diff --quiet 5f1f1c94 9973f2b3 -- corpus`).
The lane's diff touches crates/wolf_rt/src/map.rs, two test files, two
test fixtures and notes/. It touches no resolve, typecheck or WIR code.
