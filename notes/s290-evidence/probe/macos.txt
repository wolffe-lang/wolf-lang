## sess on a fresh pseudo-terminal (the program leads its session)
P start: pid==pgid yes, pid==sid yes, /dev/tty ok
P setsid by a group leader: EPERM
C1 setsid by a non-leader: ok, sid==pid yes, pgid==pid yes, /dev/tty ENXIO, tcgetpgrp(0) ENOTTY
C1 setsid again by the session leader: EPERM
C2 setsid from another thread: ok (pid), main thread's getsid(0)==pid yes, getpgrp()==pid yes, /dev/tty ENXIO
C3 open of a free pty slave by a session leader with no terminal: /dev/tty after ok
C4 TIOCNOTTY by a non-leader: ok, same session yes, /dev/tty after ENXIO

C1 setsid again by the session leader: EPERM
C2 setsid from another thread: ok (pid), main thread's getsid(0)==pid yes, getpgrp()==pid yes, /dev/tty ENXIO
C3 open of a free pty slave by a session leader with no terminal: /dev/tty after ok
C4 no terminal: ENXIO
## gsid

getsid(1): a value
getsid(reaped child): No such process
